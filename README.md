# NDSRI-Risk-Assessment-Tool
NDSRI Risk Screening Tool - A browser-based decision aid implementing ICH M7(R2) process risk factors and the FDA/EMA CPCA framework. Assesses nitrosamine formation risk and assigns potency category (1–5) with Acceptable Intake limits (18–1500 ng/day) from structural descriptors. Educational screening tool, HTML/CSS/JS.
