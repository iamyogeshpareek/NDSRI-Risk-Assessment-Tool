Scope. This tool structures the reasoning used in nitrosamine risk assessment: (1) process/formulation risk per ICH M7(R2) Step 1, and (2) a simplified educational approximation of the CPCA (Carcinogenic Potency Categorization Approach) structural logic used by FDA/EMA. It is a screening and training aid built for personal portfolio use — it is not validated software and does not replace expert structural review, read-across, (Q)SAR tools, or regulatory submission-grade assessment (e.g. Lhasa Nexus).
